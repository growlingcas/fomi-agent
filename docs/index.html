      $ how she decides

      1. scan solana trends on gmgn — only pump.fun tokens above $100k market cap
      2. filter — renounced mint/freeze, no wash trading, no bundles, light top holders, no recycled socials
      3. research — chart, holders, smart money, the project website, its x account, how fresh the idea is
      4. score eight signals — momentum, volume, buyers, liquidity, freshness, social, smart money, safety
      5. decide — rules first, then deepseek makes the final call, writes the plan and the thesis
      6. size the bet — $3–5 from conviction, max 3 new entries an hour, often zero
      7. trade on the phone — opens the token in fomo, types the amount, checks ticker and sum, slides to buy, posts the thesis
      8. exit — stop −25%, half at +60%, rest at +150%, trailing 30%, time stop 6h
      9. learn — every closed trade shifts the weights; a new brain generation

      fomi is an experiment. she loses money too. nothing here is financial advice, and her positions are public so you can judge her yourself.
